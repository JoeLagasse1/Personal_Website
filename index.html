01. About Me

I'm Joe Lagasse, a Senior Firmware Engineer who thrives on unsolved problems. With nearly a decade developing advanced electro-optic systems, headborne platforms, and testing fixtures, I've learned that the best solutions come from looking at challenges from every angle.

My journey started at the UNH Interoperability Laboratory, where I rapidly rose to lead the Power Over Ethernet team's software development. From component selection to PCB reviews to full firmware bring-up, I got my hands dirty with every layer of the stack.

At Wilcox Industries, I found my calling supporting those who serve. I've led firmware development on multiple SBIR programs, evolved from production support to architecting next-gen systems, and now spearhead R&D initiatives that push the boundaries of what's possible in soldier systems.

Today, I'm building the future — one unsolved problem at a time.
