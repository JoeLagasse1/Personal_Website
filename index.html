01. About Me

I'm Joe Lagasse, a Senior Firmware Engineer with nearly a decade of experience in the embedded software profession. I love my profession, which is both challenging and rewarding. Not only does it require technical expertise and rigor, but also the ability to look at a problem from many different angles.

I started my career in Embedded Software/Firmware Engineering during my time at the University of New Hampshire, where I worked part time during the school year at the UNH Interoperability Laboratory. While there, I rapidly rose to the lead software engineering position on the Power Over Ethernet team. We developed test boards for both Powered Devices and Power Sourcing Equipment, from design, development, and bring-up. During this process, I assisted with component selection and PCB reviews, and developed all of the firmware on the boards themselves.

After graduation, I joined Wilcox Industries to work on supporting our warfighters. I started as a Firmware Engineer I, working on test benches, production level updates, and production applications written in C#. Eventually I was able to assist on the development programs, which is where I found my true passion. It wasn't long before I was given the opportunity to lead the development of numerous Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) programs surrounding advanced Electro-Optic solutions. Eventually, I was leading all new development programs from a firmware perspective as a Senior Firmware Engineer, which required me to interact face-to-face with customers, deriving requirements from contracts, and architecting systems from the ground up.

I am now working in our new Research & Development department, spearheading Wilcox's future product lines with advanced technology and out-of-the-box thinking. Every day brings a new problem that hasn't been solved before — and that's exactly where I thrive.
